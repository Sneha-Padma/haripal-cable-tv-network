Our Service Support

Dedicated assistance for Broadband and Cable TV customers, including installation coordination, maintenance, technical assistance and customer support.

Broadband Support

We provide Broadband support for new connections, installation assistance, router and network-related support, technical troubleshooting and service coordination. If you experience a Broadband-related issue, simply connect with us and we will coordinate the required support for you.

Cable TV Support

We provide Cable TV support including service assistance, STB-related support, signal-related issues, maintenance and customer service coordination. For any Cable TV issue, contact us and our team will guide you with the necessary assistance.

Marketing & Recharge Assistance

For customers who prefer to pay their recharge or subscription amount in cash, we also provide convenient collection assistance. You do not need to search for a separate person or visit the office just for payment. Simply connect with Haripal Cable TV Network and inform us that you want to make your payment in cash. We will coordinate with our marketing team and ask them to connect with you. Our marketing representative can collect the cash from you and complete the required recharge or payment process on your behalf.
